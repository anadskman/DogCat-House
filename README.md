# Dog/Cat House

I designed a Dog and Cat house in one.
I made this for my two dogs and two cats, hence the four beds, i put a shelf at the top for the cats so they have their own space.
i also made a shelf for a food and water bowl to sit into.
The angled top is designed to open so i can fill up the water/food bowls.
there's a cavity in the walls and doors to allow insulation to be added.
Lastly I put wood panels of the faces that would be visible.
